**Bug fixes**

- `defaultArrayLength`, `recursionLimit`, and `optionalProbability` passed to `world.generate(schema, options)` were silently ignored for nested generation. All three now propagate through object field arrays and nested items via scoped `withEffective*` push/pop, matching how `store` and `locale` already behaved.
- `defaultArrayLength` was also not reaching array fields generated by the schema-based fallback (`generateZodArray`). Added `defaultArrayLength` to `GeneratorContext` so the field pipeline honours it without routing through `ctx.generate`.
- `array().min(N)` could produce fewer than N items when N exceeds `defaultArrayLength[1]` — `defMax` was incorrectly used as the lower bound of the random range.

**locale-nl: word list distribution fix**

Dutch noun and adjective lists were previously sampled as the first 5 000 / 2 000 entries of an alphabetically sorted wordlist, meaning every word started with `a`. Generation now samples evenly across the full OpenTaal wordlist and hash-shuffles the result, giving a realistic letter distribution (a:293, b:441, s:440, g:318, …).
